§7   Method notes & what brokefirst-run honesty

What the protocol caught
  1. 13 phantom-Actual rows. The guard’s first live run, and it fired immediately. The AU employment phantom alone would have inverted the entire strength table.
  2. A source conflict on policy rates. Resolved by recency with the reasoning stated, not averaged away.
  3. An unverifiable NZD rate. NZD excluded rather than guessed.
What did not work
  1. batch_run failed on both quote and ohlcv actions. Pairs had to be pulled one at a time. Only 5 of 21 watchlist pairs were read — so §4’s top two alignments carry no technical levels at all.
  2. CNN Fear & Greed geo-blocked (HTTP 451). No sentiment reading.
  3. Central bank sites not visited. §2 leans on TradingEconomics; the spec makes the CB sites authoritative for rates. That step was not executed.
  4. A date-parsing bug in the guard script (day/month transposed) threw on first run. Caught and fixed before any figure was used.
Consequence for conviction

Every §4 alignment here is fundamental-only. A real conviction call needs both halves. Treat this edition as a fundamental read with partial technical confirmation, not a complete playbook.
