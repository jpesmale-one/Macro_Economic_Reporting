Scoring method

Categories are not equally weighted — Employment and Inflation drive the rate decision, so they carry more. Within a category, a currency’s indicators split that weight evenly: US Production has 3 indicators, so each is worth 1 ÷ 3 = 0.333 (GOOD +0.333, BAD −0.333, NEUTRAL 0). AUD Employment has 3 indicators sharing weight 2 → 0.667 each.
