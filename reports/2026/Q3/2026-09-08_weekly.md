### The two collision rules, both live this week

**RULE 2 — same day, SAME reference month → share the day, averaged.**

| Day | CCY | Rows | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fri 18** | **NZD** | Balance of Trade **AUG** +1 · Inflation Rate YoY **AUG** +1 | **(+1+1)/2 = +1.00** |
| **Fri 18** | **USD** | Industrial Production **AUG** −1 · Manufacturing Production **AUG** −1 | **(−1−1)/2 = −1.00** |

**RULE 1 — same day, DIFFERENT months → the LATEST month wins.**

| Day | CCY | Rows | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wed 16** | **JPY** | Balance of Trade **AUG** *(unscorable)* · Machinery Orders YoY **JUL** −1 | **−1.00** — see below |

**⚠ THAT JPY ROW EXPOSES A GAP IN LD-98, AND I HAVE MADE A CHOICE.** The rule says the latest reference month wins the day. **It does not say what happens when the LATEST row is UNSCORABLE and an earlier one is not.** Balance of Trade AUG has no previous and no consensus; Machinery Orders JUL scores −1.

**The choice made: precedence runs over SCORABLE rows only, so the JUL row carries the day.** The alternative — the unscorable AUG row wins and blanks the day — **throws away a real signal to honour a row that carries none.** **It is a choice and it is unratified.**
