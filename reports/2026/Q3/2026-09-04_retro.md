### The finding, stated carefully

**LEADING ranked the week better than CURRENT, and CURRENT ranked it slightly WORSE THAN CHANCE.** The ordering LEADING > EQUAL > CURRENT is exactly the ordering Jeff predicted on 2 Sep from first principles.

**⚠ AND IT IS NOT STATISTICALLY MEANINGFUL. With n=8 currencies, rho needs about ±0.71 for p<0.05. All three schemes are indistinguishable from chance.** **rho +0.190 versus −0.119 is not evidence. It is one week.**

**What it IS: the first entry in a record that did not exist before 2 Sep**, and it points the way Jeff argued it would. **That is worth exactly one line in a ledger and no change to the weights.**

### VERDICT — item 6

| Sunday bucket | Currencies | Actual avg |

### VERDICT — item 2

**KEEP the cycle gate. KEEP ±50% (LD-92).** It made the correct call on the pair that lost. **Record the asymmetry** — if BUILDING fails to predict strength again next week, the gate should become a PENALTY-ONLY term rather than a symmetric multiplier.

## 3. ⚠ THE ROOM GATE (agenda item 7 — added 4 Sep)

**This is the clearest defect the week produced and it was not on the original agenda.**

| Pair | Room at selection | Room now | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NZDJPY** | 0.18 | **0.91 (2nd)** | **−324.4, 32.7 pips from failing** |
| **CADCHF** | 0.20 | **0.10 (last)** | **+33.2, the only winner** |

**The gate now ranks our worst pair second and our best pair last.**

**Why:** room measures the fraction of the 45-bar range lying in the bias direction. **NZDJPY fell to 8.7% of its range, so almost the whole range sits “above” a LONG — the gate reports the 324 pips already lost as opportunity ahead.** CADCHF rose to 89.6%, so it scores as having nowhere to go **because it worked**.

**F-69 built this to catch GBPUSD — a LONG at 94% of range with 30 pips of headroom — and it still does that correctly.** The failure is at the OTHER end of the scale, which was never tested.

### VERDICT — item 7

**DO NOT invert or down-weight room on this evidence.** The naive reading — *our winner scored 0.10, so low room is good* — is drawn from **one week in which a central bank that did not meet moved three of the eight rows.**

**SHIP ONE NARROW FIX, and only after costing it against every past selection:** room stops counting when price is **making new lows against the bias**. **Costing note: this would ALSO have removed GBPAUD** (it gained room by falling too) — and GBPAUD is not the pair that failed. **So the fix is not obviously right, and it does not ship this week.**
