Verdict

The same concentration that lost last week won this one

+144.8 pips and 3 of 3 favoured — on the identical structure that produced 0 of 3 seven days ago. All three pairs shorted AUD both weeks. AUD rose then and fell now.

That is not a vindication of the method. It is the clearest possible demonstration that a set with one shared leg is one bet, sized three times — and the variance runs both directions. GBPAUD drew down more than it ever gained and still closed positive.

The real result is the base ranking at +0.48, its best in five weeks, with three of eight currencies placed exactly and AUD correctly last. And the calibrator was subtracting from it — which is what LD-144 now fixes, measured on this week rather than argued.

The uncomfortable part is F-137. Two retrospectives were graded on the wrong price feed because I read a rule about drawings as a rule about charts, and never asked. Every macro guard passed the whole time. Nothing in this repo was watching the tape.

Directional pips are not P&L. There is no position sizing anywhere in this repo — these are the moves the reads pointed at, summed, and nothing more.
