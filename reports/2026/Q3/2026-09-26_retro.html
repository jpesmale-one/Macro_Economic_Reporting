One read, expressed three times — and this week it was the right one.

Verdict

The same concentration that lost last week won this one

+218.5 pips and 3 of 3 favoured — on the identical structure that produced 0 of 3 seven days ago. All three pairs shorted AUD both weeks. AUD rose then and fell now.

That is not a vindication of the method. It is the clearest possible demonstration that a set with one shared leg is one bet, sized three times — and the variance runs both directions.

What genuinely improved is the data. 63 of 70 rows verified against TE with zero disagreements, three new guards, and a build checklist that stops before publishing rather than after. Five of six defects this week were in the machinery, not the numbers — which is progress, because the numbers used to be the problem.

The board ranked positively for the first time (+0.12), and the base did the work (+0.29) while the calibrator subtracted (−0.29). One week ago the calibrator was the half that ranked. Four readings, no pattern — and the next real test is whether AUD staying bottom was a read or a coincidence.

Directional pips are not P&L. There is no position sizing anywhere in this repo — these are the moves the reads pointed at, summed, and nothing more.
