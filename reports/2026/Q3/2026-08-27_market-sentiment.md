### 2.7 What the score cannot see — binary thresholds

**Every threshold in §2 is BINARY, not a rate-of-change test.** A figure can
move a long way and the grade will not budge until it crosses a line — **and a
small move that DOES cross one changes the score outright.** Both halves showed
up last night.

| Row | Was | **Now** | Move | Threshold | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **USD Durable Goods Ex Transp** | 0.6% | **0.4%** | **−0.2pp** | `>0.5%` | GOOD → **BAD** |
| USD Personal Spending MoM | 0.3% | **0.2%** | −0.1pp | `0–0.5%` | NEUTRAL → **NEUTRAL** |
| AUD Inflation Rate YoY | 3.8% | **3.5%** | −0.3pp | `>2% & falling` | NEUTRAL → **NEUTRAL** |
| GBP Retail Sales Ex Fuel | 5.0% | **2.3%** | **−2.7pp** | `>0.5%` | GOOD → **GOOD** |

**Durable Goods moved 0.2pp and cost USD a full point.** GBP retail sales
**more than halved** and cost nothing. **The size of a move is irrelevant to §2
— only its position relative to a line matters.**

**A currency can weaken or firm materially for weeks while its score sits
still, and it can lose a point on a rounding-scale move.** The direction and
the magnitude are both information the score discards.

**This is why §4.6 exists**, and last night made the case twice over: **the
tally marked USD down while the dollar rallied on an off-list PCE beat**
(F-55). **§2 answers which side of the line the data is on. It is deliberately
slow, and that is what makes it a baseline rather than a signal.**

**Where it matters this week:** GBP is the LOW-confidence leg of **GBPUSD**,
and one of the two indicators behind its Spending point has decelerated sharply
while the score held. **The score says hold; the direction says watch.**

**CAD could not be updated at all.** StatCan's 21 Aug release publishes **no
year-over-year figure**, and its −0.8% July number is an **advance estimate
built from 56.5% of surveyed firms**, which StatCan states will be revised.
Complete July data lands **24 September** *(www150.statcan.gc.ca, primary)*. The
model keeps May/26 5.9% and is **flagged, not silently carried**.
