**Why a falling dollar lifts gold and BTC together, in plain terms (LD-31):**
gold and bitcoin are both priced in dollars and neither pays interest. When the
dollar falls and yields fall with it, the cost of holding a non-yielding asset
drops — you give up less by owning gold instead of a bond. That is one force
moving two assets, which is why they rose on the same day. **They are not two
independent confirmations of anything.**

**Treasury buyback expansion (19 Aug, `home.treasury.gov` sb0607)** — the
mechanism behind last week's move, and it is still operating. Treasury doubled
long-dated buyback operations from $2bn to at least $4bn, **effective 9
September**. More Treasury buying pushes bond prices up and their yields down;
lower yields make non-yielding assets cheaper to hold. **The effective date is
next month, so this is a forward-looking driver, not a spent one.**
