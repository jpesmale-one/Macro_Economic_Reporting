§4 — Method and limits

Prices are OANDA daily bars via TradingView (LD-5 as amended; Yahoo is never a fallback). Index legs are TVC and XETR. Every figure is computed at build time, not typed.

The data behind this page was wrong once, and was rebuiltThe first refresh for this build returned bars whose open had been replaced by a smoothed value equal to the bar's own low in 3667 of 8400 cases (44%%), against 122 in the committed baseline. Carried into a grading, that silently converts adverse excursion to zero and turns BEATEN days into CLEAN ones. The bars were restored from the last verified commit, re-pulled, and checked at 1%% across all 8400 before anything here was computed. pull_bars.py now refuses any pull above 20%% (F-190).
