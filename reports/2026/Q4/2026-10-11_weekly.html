The swap line is the POLICY RATE DIFFERENTIAL in the direction of the bias, not a broker swap rate. A pair has two swaps with opposite signs; which one applies depends on the side, so it is computed per position. Brokers quote swaps in points per lot, set by their own funding and marked up — two brokers differ, and so do two accounts at one broker. What is shown is the economic force underneath that number, never the number itself.

It moves nothing. LD-8 caps carry at a swing-ranking tiebreak, never a filter; this does not even tiebreak. The set, the gaps and the board are computed before it runs and do not consult it. It is disclosed so a reader holding a position for a week can see whether the week costs them anything — no lot sizes, no money figures (LD-4).

§4 Every indicator, with its working

78 rows — the evidence behind all 48 category scores.

Read a row left to right: the actual is measured against the threshold, which gives the binary +1 / 0 / −1. A category score is the mean of that currency's rows in it, times the category weight.

A Ref ending in (Q) is a QUARTERLY series. NZD publishes Unemployment and Inflation quarterly, so those rows sit two months behind the monthly ones around them — that is the cadence, not a stale row.
⚠ DIRECTION OF GOODNESS IS NOT UNIFORM Unemployment and claimant counts score +1 when they FALL — they reach the currency through the economy. Inflation scores +1 when it RISES — it reaches the currency through the central bank, and stops helping only past that bank's own ceiling. The two look alike in plain language and carry opposite signs, which is what to check first on any row that looks wrong.

The rate rows are scored from FOUR WEIGHTED TERMS, not from an actual against a threshold. score = Σ(term × weight) / Σ(weight) over the terms that exist — realised 20% · expected 30% · surprise 20% · path 30%. 60% sits on the forward half (expected + path), which is the point of a model built to foresee a move rather than report one.
A missing term drops out and the weights renormalise — a currency between meetings has no surprise, one whose next meeting is beyond the calendar horizon has no path. It is not scored 0, which would assert “no surprise” when the truth is that we cannot see one. The second line of each Terms cell shows the weights actually used.
