The Week It Paid Late

Both pairs profitable, both beaten midweek — GBPNZD closed the week at 1.98 favourable-to-adverse and NZDJPY at 1.51, but neither had it easy: each took its worst damage on Wednesday and Thursday before the risk-off turn paid them on Thursday night and Friday. The board itself was half right: rho +0.33, down from +0.48 the week before, carried entirely by the bottom of the table — AUD and NZD ranked #7 and #8 and traded #8 and #7, which is the half GBPNZD was short. The top half inverted.

§2 — The board got the bottom right and the top wrong

Spearman rho between the board's ranking and what the eight currencies actually did over the five sessions came in at +0.33, down from +0.48 on 26–30 Oct. Six of the eight sit exactly three places from where they were ranked, and the sign of that error splits the table in half: the three the board rated highest all traded lower, and the three it rated lowest all traded higher.

§4 — Method, and what this build cannot say

Every figure above is computed at build time from data/derived/sessions.csv and the OANDA daily bars in the repository, not typed. The board and the set are read from the published 2–6 Nov weekly and are never recomputed (LD-97).

This retrospective was built eleven days late, and one session nearly went unrecorded The Friday 6 Nov daily was never built at the time. It is in the record now, and this week is complete at five sessions — but the catalyst column of that daily is partial: the last usable calendar capture was taken at 15:57 PHT during the 6 Nov session, holding 11 actuals of 57 scheduled rows, so releases landing after that hour are absent rather than absent-and-marked. Two further capture attempts on 17 Nov returned pages with one table row against the usual 603; both were quarantined so an empty vintage can never be read as a real one.
The first data refresh for this build was wrong, and it would have flattered the week A full 28-pair pull returned bars whose open had been replaced by a smoothed value equal to the bar's own low in 3667 of 8400 cases (44%, against 122 in the committed baseline). Regraded on those bars, this week read ratio 2.36 instead of 1.98 and four of ten session states flipped — two BEATEN days became CLEAN and DRIFT, because an open pinned to the low means a long position never registers adverse excursion. No gate caught it: the bars were present, nothing errored, and F-188 and R-50 both passed. It was found by diffing against git. The figures in this retrospective are from the restored, verified bars.
