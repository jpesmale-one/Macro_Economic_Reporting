Was the move its own?

BROAD vs NARROWCurrency strength here is one currency against the other seven, so an average can move for two very different reasons. BROAD means it moved against nearly every counterpart — the currency itself did something. NARROW means it rose against some and fell against others, and the average moved because someone else ran. Before reaching for a catalyst, check whether there is a move to explain.
