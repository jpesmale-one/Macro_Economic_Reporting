		WHY IT LEFT THE BASE. Jeff, 2026-09-10: "GDP is a very late indicator, and
		looking at the board I'm not convinced we should add them to the tally... On
		the other hand when GDP releases the market moves."
		MEASURED as a base category it scored 6 GOOD / 2 NEUTRAL / 0 BAD with a
		weighted range of 1.00 - it gave six of eight currencies the identical +1.00
		and ranked nobody (Trade spans 4.00). But as NEWS it moves price hard: AUD
		+111.6 pips within 4h of its Q2 print, JPY +83.0 within 4h of its Q2 Final.
		GDP was not weak, it was MIS-PLACED.

		THE SCORING RULE, Jeff 2026-09-10: "GDP Consensus-Forecast is > 0 is Good,
		Consensus-Forecast is < 0 is Bad. If Consensus-Forecast is a mix of positive
		and negative values then Neutral."
		And on why it is not a surprise test: "Calibrator is forward looking and by
		the time we build the report we don't have the actual values yet. We're
		trading the expectations not the result."

		QoQ ONLY (LD-116). QoQ and YoY disagree exactly where it matters - NZ 17 Sep
		is QoQ -0.1% BAD against YoY +1.6% GOOD. QoQ is the turning point, YoY the
		accumulated past, and YoY is structurally almost always positive - which would
		recreate the very defect that removed GDP from the base.
