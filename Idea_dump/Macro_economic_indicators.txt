	6. Interest Rate - the policy rate itself. It is the quantity every other

	   EXPECTED TO DO NEXT, not on where the rate happens to sit.
		[RECORDED 2026-10-12 - LD-166. This section did not exist until now,
		 which is why Jeff had to ask "where did you get this rule?" and could
		 not check it against the spec. The rate rule had lived only in code
		 comments and git history while every other category was written here.
		 That asymmetry is the finding; the rule below is the live one.]

		a. ALL EIGHT - <Bank> Interest Rate Decision
		   THE RULE, in Jeff's words 2026-10-10:
			BEFORE THE NEWS
			1) Forecast > Previous = Good       (a hike is expected)
			2) Forecast < Previous = Bad        (a cut is expected)
			3) Forecast = Previous = Neutral    (a hold is expected)
			AFTER THE NEWS
			4) Actual > Forecast = Good         (override, hawkish surprise)
			5) Actual < Forecast = Bad          (override, dovish surprise)
			6) Actual = Forecast = retain the current assessment

		   CONSENSUS SUPERSEDES FORECAST. Jeff, 2026-10-10: "Forecast is fine
		   to use but when consensus appeared we should rescore it based on
		   that."

		   forward (LD-154b), so all eight always carry a reading rather than
		   only the three with a meeting inside TE's horizon.
